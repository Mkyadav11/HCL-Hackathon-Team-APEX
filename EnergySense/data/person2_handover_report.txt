
PERSON 2: DATABASE, DATA ENGINEERING & ANOMALY DETECTION
========================================================

DATA PREPARATION
- Selected 15 electricity-metered buildings from the Hog site.
- Data period: 2016-01-01 to 2017-12-31.
- Readings per building: 17,544 hourly observations.
- Total readings loaded into MySQL: 263,160.
- Missing, negative, and duplicate readings in the selected data: 0.

DATABASE
- Database: energy_analytics
- Tables: buildings, energy_readings, anomaly_events

ANOMALY DETECTION
- High-consumption events: 41,497.
- High-severity events: 20,485.
- Medium-severity events: 21,012.
- Flatline candidates: 2, requiring review.
- Detection rule: consumption more than 30% above baseline.
- The baseline uses median consumption grouped by building
  and hour of week.

INVESTIGATION FINDINGS
- Hog_education_Donnie had the most high-consumption events: 6,544.
- Donnie's monthly consumption rose from May and remained elevated
  through September in both 2016 and 2017.
- This suggests a recurring seasonal pattern that should be
  considered when refining anomaly detection.
- Extreme percentage deviations should be reviewed because a low
  baseline can inflate the percentage.

LIMITATIONS
- The baseline was calculated using the full two-year period,
  including observations later than the reading being assessed.
- The 30% threshold is exploratory and may flag expected seasonal
  changes as anomalies.
- Energy units have not yet been confirmed.
- Anomaly events are candidates for investigation, not confirmed faults.

EXPORTED OUTPUTS

- building_dashboard_summary.csv
